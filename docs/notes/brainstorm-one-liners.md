# Brainstorm One-Liners — Idea Generator Output

> Process (§IDEA-01 in BACKLOG.md): take a found-text list, dedupe, turn **every** line into a
> quest-theme one-liner, file into categories. Seeds only — a seed gets promoted to its own
> `§` entry + lab report before any HTML edit.
>
> Sources processed 2026-07-02: **S1** "Quiet Power" affirmations (24 lines → AFF-01..24) ·
> **S2** "That Girl" affirmations (101 lines → AFF2-01..34) · **S3** "Queen of Luxury" song
> (12 lines → AFF3-01..11). Every substantive line is mapped in a coverage table; only
> non-content artifacts ([Music], [Applause], "foreign", "thank you", timestamps, UI chrome)
> are discarded.

## Source 1 — "Quiet Power" affirmation transcript (processed 2026-07-02)

The transcript is one affirmation sequence looped ~6 times. Deduplicated: **24 unique lines,
all 24 processed below** (coverage checklist at the bottom). Two transcription variants noted
inline (AFF-09, AFF-11).

Register fit: most of these land naturally in the French-noir voice (see story.md); grace/court
seeds pair well with §DESIGN-03 Ceremonia checks; presence seeds pair with §MESH-01 co-presence.

### A. Quiet Power & Presence

- **AFF-01** — *"I move through the world with quiet power."* — The Quiet Enforcer: an escort
  quest where no blade is drawn — your accumulated mission bits (renown) suppress encounters
  on the route, and the client slowly realizes why the road went silent. Mechanic seed:
  renown-gated encounter-rate reduction (cousin of §MESH-01 sentry suppression).
- **AFF-02** — *"My presence is felt before I even speak."* — Your Story Walked Ahead: arrive
  at a new node where NPCs already greet you by deed, not name — dialogue variants keyed on
  mission bits fire *before* any introduction. Quest: find the bard who has been traveling one
  town ahead of you, retelling you.
- **AFF-21** — *"I am the calm in every room."* — The Stilled Brawl: a tavern riot that can be
  ended by combat *or* by a single Ceremonia presence check (CHA, high DC) that silences the
  room; the no-combat resolution grants a "Calm of the Room" reputation bit that changes
  tavern dialogue world-wide.
- **AFF-24** — *"I am the embodiment of grace and power."* — Two Trials, One Crown: a capstone
  with twin paths to the same reward — win a duel without taking a single hit (power), or win
  the court's midsummer dance (grace, DEX/CHA Ceremonia chain). The reward text differs by
  path; the mechanical reward doesn't.

### B. Magnetism & Attraction

- **AFF-06** — *"I don't chase, I attract."* — The Inverted Manhunt: hired to find a fugitive,
  but the winning strategy is to stop searching — make yourself conspicuously known in three
  taverns and the fugitive comes to *you*. First quest built on a lure/wait structure instead
  of fetch/kill.
- **AFF-07** — *"I am a magnet to all of my desires."* — The Lodestone Curse: a cursed item
  that raises loot drops AND encounter rate in the same breath — everything you want comes to
  you, including what wants you. Keep it or destroy it; the economy feels both choices
  (touches the §open-gaps drop-balance work).
- **AFF-10** — *"People are drawn to me without understanding why."* — The Following: strangers
  quietly accumulate behind you as you travel, node after node — a crowd tail nobody in it can
  explain. Investigation quest to find the enchantment's origin (a blessing? an old quest's
  side effect? someone else's geas mis-cast onto you?).
- **AFF-16** — *"My aura is irresistible."* — The Jealous Rival: a blessing that lowers every
  shop price and warms every greeting — and turns exactly one NPC bitterly hostile. A social
  buff with a narrative invoice; the rival's chain only exists while the aura is active.
- **AFF-23** — *"I am incredibly alluring."* — The Mark: pure noir — someone is running a
  long con and the allure aimed at you IS the weapon. Spot it with a WIS (Insight) Ceremonia
  check at any of three escalation points, or ride the branch all the way down to the sting.

### C. Stillness & Composure

- **AFF-04** — *"I am completely at ease in myself."* — The Unhaunted Mind: a monastery teaches
  the "At Ease" trait — advantage on the §DUNGEON-01 CY first-visit madness WIS save — but the
  training quest is three days of doing deliberately, provocatively nothing while the monastery
  stages temptations.
- **AFF-05** — *"My energy is calm, steady, and unshakable."* — Unshakable: earn permanent
  immunity to fear/intimidation effects by standing ground through three escalating standoffs
  without ever drawing a weapon; drawing steel at any point restarts the chain.
- **AFF-08** — *"I am comfortable in stillness."* — The Vigil: a shrine quest that requires
  literally not moving for N day ticks while events (a fleeing thief, a crying child, a fire
  rumor) tempt you off the cell. Free-Movement invariant intact: you can always walk away —
  walking away just fails the vigil. Consequence, not gate.
- **AFF-14** — *"My silence speaks volumes."* — The Interrogation: a dialogue quest where the
  winning line is no line — choose **[say nothing]** three times and the suspect fills the
  silence with the confession. First quest where silence is a first-class dialogue option.

### D. Duality & Paradox

- **AFF-03** — *"I am soft yet undeniable, mysterious yet magnetic."* — The Two-Faced Saint:
  by day a gentle healer, by night the underworld's feared arbiter — same NPC, two dialogue
  tables keyed to time-of-day. The quest is realizing they're one person, then deciding which
  half to expose. La Riva-adjacent tone (§GR).
- **AFF-13** — *"I am both warm and untouchable."* — The Beloved Projection: the kindest NPC
  in the city has never once been touched — because they are a projection, and the body
  casting it is dying somewhere in the sewers. Find the body without extinguishing the warmth
  the city depends on.
- **AFF-15** — *"My eyes hold depth and mystery."* — The Reflecting Oracle: an oracle who
  answers only through what her eyes have reflected. Fetch quest inverted into a *witness*
  quest: bring her three sights — a battle, a birth, a betrayal — by having her present
  (escort variant) or by carrying enchanted mirror-shards to each.

### E. Sovereignty & Rhythm

- **AFF-17** — *"I live by my own rhythm."* — The Town That Keeps Its Own Time: bells ring at
  impossible hours and the day tick runs visibly wrong at this node. Fix the clock — or take
  the town's side and defend its right to its own rhythm against the imperial calendar
  inspectors. (1367-friendly: calendar reform friction, see §1367.)
- **AFF-18** — *"I come and go as I please."* — The Uncageable: a thief no cell has ever held.
  You're hired not to catch them but to *design the cell that can* — and the quest forks on
  whether you build it honestly or leave the flaw you found on purpose.
- **AFF-19** — *"My world bends to make space for me."* — The Hut the Roads Avoid: the fungal
  road net (ROAD_RUNS) visibly routes around one hermit's cell — cartographers can't explain
  the detour. Lore hook that turns real map data into a mystery: why does the world bend
  around this one person?
- **AFF-20** — *"My world bends to bring everything to me."* — The Collector Who Never Leaves:
  every caravan on three roads keeps "coincidentally" diverting to one manor; its owner hasn't
  stepped outside in thirty years and owns a piece of everything. Investigate the compulsion —
  charm, contract, or something in the cellar doing the wanting.

### F. Worth, Grace & Legend

- **AFF-09** — *"I know my worth without needing to prove it."* *(one loop pass transcribes
  "without meaning to prove it" — same line, transcription drift; counted once)* — The Duelist
  Who Won't Draw: a retired legend refuses every challenge, and the town doubts the legend.
  Prove they're real *without making them fight*: a research/mission-bit collection quest
  through archives, witnesses, and an old battlefield.
- **AFF-11** — *"Every moment I make is intentional, fluid, and graceful."* *(transcript
  consistently says "moment"; almost certainly "movement" — processed as movement)* — The
  School of the Fluid Blade: a trial fight resolved entirely by a DEX Ceremonia chain instead
  of attack rolls — grace substituting for violence, round by round. Prototype for a
  skill-check combat variant (feeds §ARCH-01 W4 thinking).
- **AFF-12** — *"I exude elegance without effort."* — The Door With No Roll: court infiltration
  where the entrance check is *passive* — the doorman reads the elegance bits you've already
  earned across prior quests; there is deliberately no roll at the door. Either your history
  carries you in, or it doesn't.
- **AFF-22** — *"I am the intrigue people can't stop thinking about."* — The Rumor of You:
  your own deeds come back distorted at every tavern — the rumor engine retells you louder and
  wronger each node. Track one rumor of yourself upstream to its source, then choose: correct
  it, or embellish it and live with what that buys.

## Source 2 — "That Girl" affirmation transcript (processed 2026-07-02)

Single pass, no loop. Stripping [Music]/[Applause]/"foreign"/"thank you" artifacts leaves
**101 substantive lines** — several are fragments the artifacts cut mid-sentence (noted in
the table). All 101 are mapped below to 34 seeds (AFF2-01..34); dense litanies (the skin/hair/
body triad, the smart/talented/capable run, the closing "girl" litany) fold many lines into
one seed by design — that's the category doing its job, not a skipped line.

### A. Beauty & the Gaze

- **AFF2-01** — *gorgeous · can't keep their eyes off you · mesmerizing and magnetic ·
  breathtaking* — The Face That Stops the Street: an NPC whose beauty literally halts crowds
  mid-stride — and the pickpocket ring that works the frozen onlookers. Player-side variant: a
  glamour blessing that turns every head — perfect for creating distractions, fatal for
  tailing anyone unnoticed.
- **AFF2-02** — *"you only get prettier"* — The Portrait in Reverse: a Dorian Gray inversion —
  the noble is fine and it's the *portrait* that grows lovelier every year. Find out what,
  elsewhere in the city, is quietly paying the difference.
- **AFF2-03** — *skin / hair / body "beautiful and perfect" triad* — The Perfect Triad: an
  apothecary sells perfection in three vials — skin, hair, body. The first two do exactly what
  they promise. The third vial's price is the quest.
- **AFF2-04** — *scent is enchanting · always smell amazing* — The Enchanted Scent: a perfumer
  binds a signature scent to you — allies' hounds can track you anywhere (rescue asset), and
  so can everything else with a nose. One blessing, two edges.
- **AFF2-05** — *"your body language is always perfect and alluring"* — The Unreadable Tell:
  a card-den arc where your composure is *too* perfect — the house concludes you must be
  cheating precisely because you have no tell.

### B. Charisma & Social Gravity

- **AFF2-06** — *exude elegance and confidence (cf. AFF-12) · "pretty privilege is a thing and
  you have it"* — Pretty Privilege: a world-effect blessing — guards wave you through, prices
  soften, doors open — until you meet the one NPC immune to it, who becomes the only
  questgiver who actually *sees* you.
- **AFF2-07** — *"you always inspire those around you"* — The Inspiring Banner: earnable
  "Inspiring" tag — co-located allies fight at +1 (natural flavor for the §MESH-01
  co-presence buff); the earning quest is talking a broken militia back onto the wall.
- **AFF2-08** — *"you are witty and intellectual"* — The Salon Duel: a battle of wits with a
  court poet run as an INT/CHA Ceremonia riposte chain — lose a round, lose a reputation bit;
  win the salon, win a patron.
- **AFF2-09** — *"you light up every room"* — The Lit Room: rooms have a mood state and your
  entrance flips it — the deliberate inverse of AFF-21's calm. Festival quest where the only
  way to flush out the conspirators is to make the whole hall too bright and loud to whisper in.
- **AFF2-10** — *no one can help but love you · a blessing in anyone's life* — The Compulsory
  Love: everyone loves you — including the judge trying your case and the general sworn to
  your enemy. Love as complication; determine whether it's a gift or a planted curse before
  it gets someone hanged for favoring you.
- **AFF2-11** — *luxury to talk to you / have your time / be in your space / be in your
  circle* — The Velvet Circle: your attention becomes a court currency — NPCs queue and pay
  for audiences. Someone is buying up access to you wholesale; find out what they're really
  purchasing.
- **AFF2-12** — *your energy is magnetic (cf. AFF-16) · "No One Compares To You"* — The
  Incomparable: a jealous rival commissions a doppelganger of you. It's flawless — and fails
  in exactly one way. The quest is discovering which way, before it finishes replacing you.

### C. Luck & Providence

- **AFF2-13** — *magnet for all that is good · everything works in your favor · things
  magically work out · so lucky · luckiest girl in the world · amazing things always
  happening* — The Luck Vein: a person around whom probability visibly bends — perfect
  weather, crit fish bites, enemy misfires. Gamblers and generals both want her. Core
  question the arc keeps asking: is luck zero-sum — and if so, who's paying?
- **AFF2-14** — *it feels so good to be you · so fun to be you · your life is the best* — The
  Envied Life: someone loves your life enough to steal it wholesale — an identity-magic con
  that swaps reputations first, faces second. Defend the joy of being you, literally.
- **AFF2-15** — *the universe spoils you · truly blessed and abundant in all areas* — The
  God-Favored: a temple publicly declares one citizen "favored of the gods" and the tithes
  pour in. Determine whether the favor is real, a racket, or — worst case — real *and* a
  racket.

### D. Wealth & Ease

- **AFF2-16** — *money always available · money just handed to you · bills always paid ·
  money just loves you (cf. AFF3-03) · spend and receive the same amount back* — The Purse
  That Refills: gold spent returns by morning, every morning. Somewhere a ledger is bleeding
  by exactly that amount; trace the refill upstream. (Pairs with the §MESH-01.7 mint-id
  ledger lore — every coin has a lineage.)
- **AFF2-17** — *life filled with ease · nothing is hard for you · life on easy mode* — Easy
  Mode: a town where nothing ever goes wrong — encounters simply don't fire on its cells —
  and whose people have quietly forgotten how to cope with anything. Discover what eats the
  hardship, and what it's owed.
- **AFF2-18** — *always taken care of · love being taken care of and spoiled · showered with
  money and gifts so you never have to worry* — The Patron: an anonymous benefactor pre-pays
  your inns, settles your debts, tips your enemies to leave town. Find your patron; refusing
  the care is a real branch with real consequences.

### E. Boundaries & Self-Respect

- **AFF2-19** — *strong boundaries · easy to uphold your boundaries · you respect yourself ·
  you love yourself* — The Warded Heart: boundaries literalized — a hedge-witch teaches a
  personal ward that physically stops unwanted approach (blocks pickpockets and charm
  effects). The quest to learn it is a week of saying no to escalatingly sympathetic asks.
- **AFF2-20** — *you are respected · because you carry yourself so well* — Carriage: how you
  walk is read — travel-style choices (stride into town vs slip in) shift NPC greeting tiers
  at arrival. Small systemic seed, big flavor.
- **AFF2-21** — *know and determine your worth · never have to prove yourself · more than
  enough as you are (cf. AFF-09)* — The Unproven: extends AFF-09's Duelist Who Won't Draw —
  a quest that rewards you specifically for *declining* the proving grounds; the reward bit
  only sets if you never once rolled to impress.
- **AFF2-22** — *"you put yourself on the pedestal"* — The Self-Made Statue: a sculptor
  offers to carve living heroes. Commission your own likeness or decline; the statue (or the
  empty plinth) permanently changes that node's dialogue about you.
- **AFF2-23** — *easy to ask for what you want · know exactly what you want and need* — The
  Plain Ask: a noir negotiation web where every scheme branch dead-ends and the winning move
  is the one nobody in the genre ever tries — walking in and asking directly. The anti-
  intrigue quest.

### F. Health, Discipline & Romanticized Life

- **AFF2-24** — *love taking care of yourself · nourishing foods your body loves · blessed
  with great health · love living healthy · mind and body resilient · taking care of body
  and mind* — The Regimen: the game's first daily-habit loop — a trainer NPC whose program
  (eat, rest, train, per day tick) builds a stacking "Resilient" trait (+HP regen). Skip
  days and it decays; discipline as mechanic, not lecture.
- **AFF2-25** — *"you are a disciplined woman"* — The Blade Convent: a martial order whose
  entire syllabus is refusal — of shortcuts, of provocation, of the third drink. Feeds the
  AFF-05 Unshakable chain.
- **AFF2-26** — *love romanticizing your life · see beauty in everything · in love with
  life* — The Romanticist: a painter who finds beauty in mud, monsters, and ruin; escort her
  sketching expeditions into dangerous cells. Her finished paintings re-write the flavor text
  of the monsters she witnessed — the bestiary itself gets romanticized.

### G. Freedom & Possibility

- **AFF2-27** — *freely do what you desire · go wherever and do whatever you want · never
  have to fear, opportunities always available* — The Church of the Open Road: a sect that
  worships free movement itself — no gate may bar a pilgrim, only consequence may follow one.
  (The Free-Movement design invariant, canonized in-world.)
- **AFF2-28** — *successful in anything · smart · talented · capable · ambitious · anything
  you dream of · powerful · limitless ("you are littlest" — transcription garble)* — The
  Prodigy: a young NPC who is good at *everything*, which means every guild in the city is
  bidding to own her future. Protect the one thing talent can't buy her: the right to choose.
- **AFF2-29** — *"effortlessly" (orphaned fragment — its clause was cut by the [Music]
  marker)* — The Effortless: a swordmaster whose secret is doing strictly less than every
  opponent; teaches a stance that converts unspent movement into defense. A fragment of a
  line, so: a fragment of a style.

### H. Love & Partnership

- **AFF2-30** — *surrounded by amazing people · surrounded by love* — Good Company: your
  companion NPCs start knowing *each other* — inter-NPC scenes unlock when the right pair is
  co-located. The social web as content, not backdrop.
- **AFF2-31** — *only accept healthy and stable loving relationships · healthy love comes
  naturally* — The Standard: a matchmaker who refuses to make bad matches — and the furious
  powerful client she just turned down. Defend her standard in court(ship). Yael-arc adjacent
  (§DESIGN-03 romance register).
- **AFF2-32** — *loved and appreciated · worthy of so much love · receiving so much love ·
  lovable and amazing* — The Gratitude Ledger: everyone you've ever helped writes a line in a
  village book — a mission-bit-driven epilogue generator that reads your actual play history
  back to you as testimony.
- **AFF2-33** — *everyone's dream partner · an amazing partner* — The Hundred Suitors: a
  courtship siege — a hundred proposals camped outside one farmhouse. Resolve it without a
  single duel (or fail into all of them).

### I. The Girl Litany

- **AFF2-34** — *dream girl · hot girl · wealthy girl · lovable girl · successful girl ·
  spoiled girl · radiant girl · glowing girl · beautiful girl · **that girl*** — The Ten
  Titles: a fairy court offers ten titles, each a small distinct boon — but only one may be
  worn. "That Girl" is the hidden eleventh, offered only to a player who refuses the first
  ten. Litany as boon-selection screen.

### Wealth-Magic & Luxury (single category — it's one song, one theme)

- **AFF3-01** — *"I am rich."* — The Rich Beggar: a beggar who states, flatly, that he is
  rich — and node by node the claim checks out (a deed here, a vault key there, a name that
  opens doors). Identity-as-wealth investigation; the twist is why a rich man begs.
- **AFF3-02** — *"I attract money."* — The Coin-Drift: in one merchant's shop, dropped coins
  visibly roll toward the counter — always toward the counter. Measure it, map it, find the
  lodestone heart under the floorboards. Physics-of-greed vignette.
- **AFF3-03** — *"Money loves me."* — The Imprinted Mint: a mint whose coins imprint on their
  first owner like ducklings — stolen coins crawl home by night. Theft-proof currency, a
  thieves' guild in crisis, and a fence who's found the one way to launder a loyal coin.
  (The §MESH-01.7 mint-id/lineage ledger made literal in-world.)
- **AFF3-04** — *"I feel sunny."* — The Sunny One: an NPC whose mood IS the local weather —
  the node has rained for three years. Cheer her up and the map cell's weather state changes
  permanently. Weather as quest output.
- **AFF3-05** — *"I am beauty." / "I am light."* — The Twin Embodiments: two shrine spirits,
  Beauty and Light, each claiming the town needs only her. Carry one candle through one dark
  dungeon and you'll have data for the debate.
- **AFF3-06** — *"Wealth and love are mine tonight."* — Tonight Only: one festival night when
  every bet pays and every courtship lands — and everything won reverts at dawn unless sealed
  (spent, vowed, or given away). A timed day-tick window quest about what you keep.
- **AFF3-07** — *"I am beautiful. I am rich." (the chant pair)* — The Mantra Cult: a
  congregation chanting wealth into being — and the gold really appears. Conservation-of-gold
  noir: the region's purses are lightening by exactly the take. Find the pipe, not the miracle.
- **AFF3-08** — *"Life is magic. Watch it switch."* — The Switch: a stage magician whose
  signature trick actually swaps two things permanently each show — two house keys, two
  names, two fates. Nobody notices until you do. Undo the switches, in show order.
- **AFF3-09** — *"Every moment gold finds me."* — Gold Finds Her: a chosen one gold literally
  seeks — coins in gutters, crows dropping rings, purses splitting at her step — while
  everyone within a mile grows poorer by precisely that much. Zero-sum providence (the dark
  mirror of AFF2-13's Luck Vein).
- **AFF3-10** — *"I am the queen of luxury."* — The Queen of Luxury: a self-crowned queen
  with no land, no army — only splendor, and a court that runs entirely on credit. The quest
  arrives with the bills. What does a kingdom of luxury do at the reckoning?
- **AFF3-11** — *"Money flows, beauty glows. Everywhere my power shows."* — The Three Pillars:
  an enchantress whose power stands on visible wealth (the flow), visible beauty (the glow),
  and visible reputation (the show) — sever any pillar in any order and the other two weaken.
  Three-pillar boss design; order chosen by the player changes which finale fires.
