Pass 4 on ATH-iliad.md added five source summaries (Parts 1–5 of the Butler translation) and five new vignette cycles (Cycles 8–12), completing the ATH quest chain. Cycle 8, "Apollo's Account," builds a quest around the plague tally tablet kept by the Tenedos sanctuary servant — the nine-day count of mules, dogs, and men that precedes the poem's first line. Cycle 9, "Dolon's Report," follows the one complete intelligence document of the war: the spy's voluntary field briefing given in the dark moments before Diomedes cuts his throat, accurate in every particular, recorded on ox-hide by a Myrmidon scribe. Cycle 10, "The Stripped Inventory," tracks the clay tablet an Achaean armorer made of Achilles's divine armor after Hector stripped it from Patroclus's body — the chain Hephaestus to Peleus to Achilles to Patroclus to Hector — with a fisherman's testimony attached about how divine armor looks on a man it was not made for. Cycle 11, "Priam's Ransom," takes the grief-ledger Idaeus the herald made the morning after the ransom: twelve vestments, twelve cloaks, twelve rugs, ten talents of gold, the Thracian cup; gold weighed by the father himself in the dark with the sons dismissed from the courtyard; Hecuba's dissent appended at the bottom in her own hand. Cycle 12, "Thetis at the Sea-Floor," is the closing cycle of the entire ATH chain: a sealed sea-clay cylinder left on a rock at Tenedos by a Nereid, containing a bronze strip on which Thetis recorded chronologically every act she performed for Achilles from the quarrel to the burial in the golden urn she had carried from Olympus since he was a child without telling him what it was for.
