Pass 4 on Jerusalem Delivered appended four source summaries and four new vignette cycles (8–11) to JRS-jerusalem-delivered.md. Cycle 8 (*The Desertion Record*) centers on Godfrey's muster-roll of the forty-three knights who followed Armida — a document no one wanted to create, preserved because Godfrey wrote on it in his own hand "Let this remain unburned. They were not cowards," and which forces a new archive category for absence under magical compulsion. Cycle 9 (*The Physician's Account*) follows the case notebook of Erotimus, the surgeon who tried and failed to extract the arrow from Godfrey's leg and wrote, without interpretation, the clinical notation that the arrowhead left the wound without any instrument or visible hand — a document the Jerusalem relics registry wants to claim and the archive must protect as a civil medical record. Cycle 10 (*The Siege Engineer's Account*) preserves the field report of the officer who sent three timber-cutting expeditions into Ismen's enchanted forest and wrote on the final line "I cannot account for this by the principles of my science. I report it as I observed it," requiring a new archive category for professional reports that reach the limit of their discipline. Cycle 11 (*The Preaching Record*, questComplete: true) works with the wax tablet a chaplain filled with partial shorthand during Peter the Hermit's sermon before the final assault — a tablet with deliberate gaps where the pen could not keep pace — and establishes the archive principle that the gaps are evidence, not damage, and must be filed whole alongside witness testimony as two cross-referenced documents rather than one completed unit.
