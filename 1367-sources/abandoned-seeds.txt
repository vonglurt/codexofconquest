## How to read this file

When a user selects 1 of 4 angle options for a cycle 2 quest, the 3 rejected options
are logged here. They are NOT deleted from the source file — they remain in the
"Seeds" section of the relevant source file. This file tracks what was explicitly
considered and passed over during human review, so future sessions can:
  (a) avoid re-proposing the same seeds as if new
  (b) consider whether any rejected seed deserves reconsideration for cycle 3+
  (c) understand the shape of human preference across the pipeline

## Notes on the selection pattern

Looking at the choices made across this pipeline, the human consistently chose:
- Seeds with a **single, named human subject** at the ethical center
  (Draupadi, Sita, Mina, Ramon de Cortés + Don Alonso, Fiamma dei Rondinelli)
- Seeds where the **TOKEN has a clear philosophical identity**, not just material value
- Seeds that **extend the archive's categorization capabilities** — each choice created
  a new Sweelinck category that didn't exist before
- Seeds with **routes that went to WM** (the archive endpoint) — the carrier's journey
  ends at the institution, not mid-road

Seeds that were rejected tended to be:
- Primarily **logistical** rather than philosophical (HAV-02 Sunk Boat, HAV-03 Log)
- **Internally complete** without requiring the archive as an institution to process them
- Routes that ended in a city other than WM without a strong reason

This pattern suggests: for cycle 3+ proposals, prioritize seeds where the archive
creates something new by receiving the document, not just stores something old.
